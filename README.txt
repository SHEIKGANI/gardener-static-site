GREENSCAPE - STATIC GARDENING & LANDSCAPING WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: page structure and content
- style.css: responsive styling
- script.js: mobile navigation, current year, demo email enquiry form

Run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in a browser, or use VS Code Live Server.
3. Replace the GreenScape name, phone number, email, copy and Unsplash image URLs with your own details.

Contact form:
The current form uses a mailto link and opens the visitor's email app. To receive enquiries directly on your website, connect a backend or a form service.

Images and Google Fonts:
The demo loads images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts, so an internet connection is needed for those assets.
